Signs your door needs John

Garage doors rarely fail without warning. They complain first, like this:

It groans and grindsGrinding or squeaking usually means rollers, hinges or a dry track asking for attention.
It moves unevenlySluggish, jerky or crooked travel points to springs or cables wearing out on one side.
It will not open or closeA dead opener, a snapped spring or a tripped sensor. All fixable, most of them the same day.
The remote has a mind of its ownInconsistent response is often a simple sensor or programming fix, not a new opener.
