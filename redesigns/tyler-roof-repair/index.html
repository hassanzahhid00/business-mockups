The founder

The man behind the ladder.

“I started this company because I was tired of watching homeowners get pushed into roofs they did not need.”

Justin Deason founded Tyler Roof Repair on a simple frustration: in this trade the easy money is in full replacements, and too many homeowners get sold one when a proper repair would do. That is not how he does business.

Every inspection is a straight-up assessment. His crew shows you exactly what they find, explains what actually needs work, and gives you the honest answer even when it costs them a sale. With over 20 years working East Texas roofs, Justin knows the heat, the hail, and the materials that hold up to both.
