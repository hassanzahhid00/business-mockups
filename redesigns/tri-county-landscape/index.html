About Us

Local Crews, Natural Methods

Tri-County Landscape is a Cape Coral company that has spent more than two decades looking after properties from Captiva Island to Naples. Our trained teams handle everything from a single tree to full community maintenance contracts.

We work with natural materials and methods wherever we can, avoiding harsh chemicals and invasive species around Florida homes, so your yard stays safe for kids, pets and the local ecosystem. You do not even need to be home while we work, we handle the job and leave the property clean.

Getting started is simple: request a free estimate, we inspect the property in person, agree the work, and our crew completes it. One-time payments and recurring plans are both available.
