What we do

Pruning, explained.

Hand Pruning of Shrubs

Shrubs shaped and thinned by hand, one cut at a time, so each plant keeps its natural form and vigor instead of being sheared into a ball.

/ 02

Small Tree Pruning

Careful work on small trees: clearing what is dead or crossing, and guiding the canopy so the tree grows into its space, not against it.

Pruning Consultation

A walk through your garden with a plan: what to prune, what to leave alone, and how to manage the landscape over the long term.

02
The approach

Purposeful. Proactive.

Pruning is not a haircut. It shapes how a plant grows, heals and ages for years after the cut is made.

/ 01Form and vigor, balanced

Every job weighs the plant's natural form and energy against the space it actually has to grow in.

/ 02Health before size

A cut changes a plant's physiology, not just its outline. We prune for the health of the whole plant, not the quickest tidy-up.

/ 03Thought about the site

Species, soil, light and the age of the landscape all feed into the plan before a single cut is made.

/ 04Long-term management

Gardens and landscapes are managed over seasons and years, not fixed in a single visit.

The pruner

Meet Anna Hauser.

Good pruning unites critical thinking with a real affection for woody plants.

Anna Hauser founded Fine Pruning in 2008 and still does the work herself. She trained in the Professional Gardener Training Program at Longwood Gardens and worked in horticulture and organic agriculture in Ireland and Italy before taking an honors degree at the University of Georgia.

She is an ISA Certified Arborist, and later added a Masters in City and Regional Planning from Georgia Tech. That mix of field craft and planning is exactly what she brings to a garden: hands that know the cut, and a head thinking years ahead of it.
