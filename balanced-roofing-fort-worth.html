Can hail really damage a roof without leaking?

Yes. Hail bruises and cracks shingles long before water gets in, which is why a drone inspection matters: the damage is documented while your claim window is still open.

We do. We walk the roof with your inspector and the drone documentation in hand, so damaged areas are not overlooked.
